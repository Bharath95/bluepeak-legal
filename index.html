WingIt

One tick, a thousand wings.

WingIt: To-Do List & Reminders is a gentle to-do app where every task you finish becomes wind beneath a shared flock of migratory birds — real species, flying real routes, carried by the quiet effort of people around the world.

In closed beta on Google Play

The app is currently in closed testing with the public Google Play listing launching shortly at:
play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bluepeak.wingit

What WingIt does

  • A clear, calm to-do list — quick capture, repeating tasks, daily reminders
  • Every finished task visibly helps a shared flock migrate across painted skies
  • Guilt-free by design: no streaks, no scores, no penalties — only lift
  • Private by design: task content is never shown to anyone else
  • Optional in-app purchases (cosmetic "Sponsor the sky") processed via Google Play
