WingIt — Privacy Policy

Effective 13 August 2026 · App: WingIt: To-Do List & Tasks (com.bluepeak.wingit) · Developer: BluePeak software

WingIt is a to-do app where finishing your own tasks helps a shared, anonymous flock of birds migrate. Privacy is a founding rule of the product: the content of your tasks is never shown to, or shared with, any other user. Other travelers only ever see anonymous, aggregate signals such as distance flown and weather.

Information we collect

  • Account details (optional). If you sign in with Google or email, we store your email address and, for Google accounts, your display name. You can use WingIt without an account; in that case your data stays on your device.
  • Your tasks. Task titles, reminders, and completion state are stored on your device and — when you're signed in — synced to our cloud database so you can restore them.
  • Aggregate activity. Completing tasks contributes anonymous counters (e.g. kilometres of "wind") to the shared journey. These counters contain no task content and cannot be traced back to you by other users.
  • Purchases (when available). Optional cosmetic purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no sale of personal data — ever.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No sharing of task content with anyone: not other users, not partners.

Where data is stored

Synced data is hosted on Supabase (managed Postgres) and protected with per-user access rules; data travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Sign-in is handled by Google and by Supabase Auth.

Notifications

Reminders you set are scheduled locally on your device. We don't send marketing notifications.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all synced data from our servers, immediately. Signing out removes your data from the device. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

Children

WingIt is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.
