Delete your WingIt account and data

App: WingIt: To-Do List & Tasks (com.bluepeak.wingit) · Developer: BluePeak software

Delete from inside the app (immediate)

  1. Open WingIt and sign in if you aren't already.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and, for Google sign-in, your display name).
  • All your synced tasks, reminders, and completion history.
  • Your postcards and personal journey records.
  • All data stored about you on your device.

What is kept

Only anonymous aggregate counters (for example, the total kilometres the shared flock has flown), which contain no personal information and cannot be traced back to you. There is no additional retention period for personal data.
